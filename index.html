Hello! I’m a PhD candidate in Mathematics at the University of California, San Diego. My advisor is Bennett Chow.

Prior to starting at UC San Diego, I received my MS in Mathematics in 2020 from New York University's Tandon School of Engineering, where my thesis advisor was Yisong Yang. I received my BA in Mathematics from UC Berkeley in 2015.

Research interests

My current work is in geometric analysis—specifically, generalizing results on manifold Ricci flow to orbifold Ricci flow. I am also interested in applications of geometry to physics. For more, see here.

Teaching

I recently taught Precalculus as part of UC San Diego’s Summer Graduate Teaching Scholars program for 2026, and have been a Teaching Assistant for various other courses, listed here.
